# Skala Brand System v0

## Brand idea

Skala is a premium, AI-native system for building business mastery.

The name should evoke progression, scale, levels and an expanding field of expertise without explaining the product literally.

## Brand personality

- Intelligent
- Precise
- Ambitious
- Contemporary
- Slightly futuristic
- Professional without feeling corporate
- Challenging without being academic or intimidating

Avoid:

- generic edtech friendliness;
- consultant aesthetics;
- MBA clichés;
- graduation caps, books, lightbulbs or brains;
- generic AI stars/sparkles;
- cyberpunk neon overload;
- childish gamification.

## Positioning language

Working descriptors:

- `The AI-native business school.`
- `Business, mastered.`
- `Build your business expertise.`
- `The knowledge of an MBA, rebuilt for the AI era.`

The brand name itself should not be permanently locked to “MBA.” MBA can be a powerful acquisition/category wedge while Skala remains the masterbrand.

## Visual system

### Base palette

- `--bg: #0A0B0D`
- `--surface: #121419`
- `--surface-raised: #191C22`
- `--text: #F2F1EC`
- `--text-muted: #8E929C`
- `--signal: #C7FF4A`
- `--intelligence: #8B7CFF`

### Signal logic

Lime is not decoration. It communicates:

- progress;
- validated evidence;
- mastery;
- newly available paths;
- active primary actions when appropriate.

Violet communicates contextual intelligence:

- Skala interventions;
- generated explanation;
- adaptive challenge;
- AI-supported analysis.

## Graphic motif

Explore a progression/scale motif without literal stairs:

`▁ ▂ ▃ ▅ ▇`

Possible applications:

- wordmark companion;
- loading state;
- mastery indicator;
- graph cluster density;
- motion system;
- case progress.

The graph itself is a major brand asset: a sparse constellation of expertise where learned areas illuminate over time.

## Typography

Use three restrained layers:

1. Grotesk/sans for product UI.
2. Editorial serif for case titles and high-attention narrative moments.
3. Monospace for identifiers, scores and system-like data.

Do not overuse the serif or monospace; they create contrast, not separate visual brands.

## Image direction

Prefer:

- abstract systems;
- diagrams;
- business data;
- editorial typography;
- subtle spatial/constellation imagery;
- minimal real-world imagery only when it materially grounds a case.

Avoid stock business photography.

## Voice

Concise, direct and intellectually confident.

Good:

`Your recommendation assumes acquisition costs stay flat.`

`What changes if CAC rises 35%?`

`AI Strategy +4 mastery`

Bad:

`Amazing work! You're absolutely crushing it! 🎉`

`Let's unlock your limitless potential!`

## Product naming vocabulary

Preferred:

- Your Skala
- Business Core
- Domain
- Specialization
- Skill
- Case
- Challenge
- Mastery
- Evidence
- Perspective

Avoid as primary product language:

- Course
- Lesson 12
- Student
- Homework
- Quiz
- Certificate
- XP

Those terms can appear when semantically necessary, but should not define the system.
